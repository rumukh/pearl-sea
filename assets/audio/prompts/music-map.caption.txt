Instrumental music for a children's undersea adventure game: sailing across the sea map in a little yellow submarine and choosing where to dive next. Curious, adventurous and light: a playful pizzicato strings and flute melody over a gentle rocking sailing rhythm with ukulele, marimba, a soft round upright bass and light hand percussion, with little harp glissandos and glockenspiel sparkles like rising bubbles. Bright major key, moderate tempo, steady even energy with no big build-ups, no breakdowns and no drops. Clean, warm mix with gentle reverb. The groove keeps going from start to end so the music loops smoothly.
