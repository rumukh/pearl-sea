Instrumental cartoon battle music for a children's undersea adventure game, where a grumpy, inky sea monster is washed clean with bubbles. Exciting, bouncy and mischievous but never scary or dark: fast staccato pizzicato strings and bassoon, a playful tuba bass line, xylophone runs, punchy timpani and tom hits, short bright brass stabs, swirling flute trills and bubbly synth accents. Minor key with a cheeky, funny-spooky flavour, a driving energetic groove that stays at the same intensity without long breaks or quiet passages. Bright, clean orchestral mix. The groove keeps going from start to end so the music loops smoothly.
