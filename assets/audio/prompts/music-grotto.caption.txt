Instrumental music for a children's undersea adventure game: the cosy home grotto of a little yellow submarine, where friendly sea creatures live. Warm, cheerful and gently playful, a happy coming-home feeling. A soft marimba and vibraphone play a simple, catchy, singable melody over plucked ukulele chords, a round warm upright bass, light shaker and soft brushed snare, with little celesta and glockenspiel sparkles and airy, bubbly synth plucks. Bright major key, relaxed bouncy groove, steady even energy with no big build-ups, no breakdowns and no drops. Clean, warm, intimate mix with gentle reverb. The groove keeps going from start to end so the music loops smoothly.
